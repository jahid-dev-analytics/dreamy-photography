Where they actually conflict. A and C both put the site in front of the picture — A with type, C with grading and 3D. B is the only one that lets a photograph arrive uninterpreted, and it is also the one that dies if the pictures are ordinary. The likely merge is B’s grid and single-face discipline carrying C’s black stage for the series view, with A’s masthead demoted to a small wordmark. Tell me if that is the trade you want, or pick one outright.
